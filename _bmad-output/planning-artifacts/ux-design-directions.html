"My birds run loose under the live oaks. The eggs taste like that — like a place, not a brand."
— Frank, on the farm since 2018

Frank Reyes started with six hens behind his house in 2018. Eight years later, his flock is thirty strong, all free-range under a stand of mature live oaks at the back of the property. Every bird has a name, although Frank admits "they don't always come when called."

The hens forage for insects, scratch grass, and dust-bathe in the shade. Their feed is supplemented but never the main course — that's why the yolks come up the color of marigolds and the whites stand tall. Pickup at the farm is Saturday mornings; Frank's wife usually pulls them out of the cooler with the order number written in pencil on the carton.

Frank's farm is permitted under the FL Limited Poultry/Egg Farm Operation regulation and certified Animal Welfare Approved by A Greener World — both verified through Farm2Table.
